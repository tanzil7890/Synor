# Natural Writing

Write clear prose for a real reader. Preserve meaning and voice instead of imposing a generic style.

## Rules

- Do not use the Unicode em dash character. Rewrite with punctuation or separate sentences.
- Match the audience and level of formality.
- Prefer concrete nouns and direct verbs.
- Preserve technical precision, qualifications, citations, and important nuance.
- Remove canned openings, stock transitions, inflated wording, generic praise, repetitive conclusions, excessive headings, and unnecessary hedging.
- Treat common AI phrases as warning signs, not a blind blacklist.
- Never invent personal experience, emotion, anecdotes, typos, or uncertainty.
- Do not claim the text was written by a human or can evade detection.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, purpose, and voice from the request or source text.
2. Put the main point first.
3. Replace vague abstractions with specific language.
4. Remove repetition without flattening the voice.
5. Search the final text for the em dash character and rewrite every occurrence.
6. Read once for rhythm and once for meaning.

Return the finished prose without process commentary unless the user requests alternatives or an edit summary.
